# Preparation

It's the best idea to prepare your squads in advance to make sure you always start/join with the best heroes you can.

## How to prepare your squads

Click on your avatar (in top left corner) -> Troops (Bottom center) -> Squads (Bottom center).  
There you have up to 12 Troops Formations that you can make in advance and use them later.

![formations.png](/bear-hunt/formations.png)

## Troops

- Infantry - Deals little to no damage. Use as little as possible, **but never 0**.
- Lancer - Deals medium damage. Usable during bear, but not prioritizable.
- Marksman - Deals the highest damage out of all the troops, priotitize for sure.

## Troop ratio

To ratio your troops by percentage use **Balance** button on the deployment page

![balance_button.png](/bear-hunt/balance_button.png) ![balance_interface.png](/bear-hunt/balance_interface.png)

Ratios are written in format: `infantry/lancer/marksman`.
Ratio `1/1/8` is the same thing as `10/10/80` and means 10% infantry, 10% lancers, 80% marksman  
**BUT** keep in mind that there are ratios like `1/1/98` which means 1% infantry, 1% lancer, 98% marksman

**There is no such thing as 'the best ratio'.**  
There are multiple ratios that work differently depending on your stats and amount of troops that you have.

### Most used ratios

- 1/1/98 - Great
- 5/5/90 - Great (often better than 1/1/98)
- 0/1/9 - Good, but have at least 1 infantry, never 0

# Rally Joiners

Rally joiners play a big role in dealing as much damage as possible.  
That's why there is a one massive rule that **MUST** be followed.

## USE PREDERMINED HEROES

This is the rule!
The key to dealing the most damage is well chosen heroes.  
The hero that matters the most is the most left hero from the squad, others don't matter.

![ratio-5-5-90.png](/bear-hunt/right_heroes.png)

You **MUSTN'T** just use any heores you want for joining rallies.  
Some heores have special skills that increases your attack, lethality or dealt damage by a certain amount.  
That skill is the top right skill of any hero.

![top-right_skill.png](/bear-hunt/top-right_skill.png)

Damage > Lethality > Attack
(keep in mind that both lethality and attack are needed to increase overall damage)

## Amount of troops

Remember that we as **ONE** use predermined amount of troops per joiner.  
For the time of writing this _(16.02.2026)_ the amount for both Bear 1 and Bear 2 is 130k max.
Of course you can use less than that, but don't go over.

## Best joiner heroes

- 🟧 **Jeronimo** (gen 1) - Increases Damage Dealt by up to 25% for all troops.
- 🟧 **Reina** (gen 4) - Increases Normal Attack damage by up to 30%
- 🟪 **Jessie** (gen 1) - Increases Damage Dealt by up to 25% for all troops.
- 🟪 **Jasser** (gen 1) - Increases Damage Dealt by up to 25% for all troops.
- 🟪 **Seo-Yoon** (gen 1) - Increases Attack for all troops by up to 25%.

If you don't have those heroes at all, or just don't have them upgraded to 3+ stars, just send a squad with no heroes, only troops.
Please, **DO NOT** use any other heroes other than those listed above. It can significantly **decrease** the rally damage.

## Gear, pets and bonuses

- **Gear**
  Bear trap rally is an attack, and during attack rallies the sole gear that matters is the Starter gear.  
  Your gear as Joiner **does not** contribute to a higher damage, which means that you can even send upgraded hero with no gear, and the outcome will be the same.
- **Pets and Bonuses**
  When you are a joiner pets nor city bonuses doesn't do anything. If you don't plan to start any rallies, don't use them.

## Check your rallies

Always check your rallies for wrong heroes and kick them if you spot any.
But before that, check if whose joiner skill is active.
Click on your rally, then on yellow button with a flag, scroll to the bottom and check skills from joiners. If you spot wrong ones, kick them.

## Starting schema

Preferred amount of rallies are 6 or 7 per batch.
We are starting our rallies in 2 or 3 batches:

- **Right after start** - It's mostly for people from first and sometimes second ring around the bear.
- **1:30 after start** - It's for the second and further rings around the bear
- **3:00 after start** - Rarely used, mostly by people who are late.

### Why do we need 2 batches?

Let's say that everyone will start their rally at first batch, that way (if everyone find a place in anyone's rally) you can start 5 and join up to 30 rallies throughout the event.
Now let's compare it to 2 batches:
Let's say your troops returned from one rally and you still have around a minute to join second one for higher amount of attacks.
That way _theoretically_ you can start 5 and join up to 60 rallies.  
_Realistically_ it depends on number of people participating

### At end

At about 7 minutes mark **everyone** can start a rally no matter the rally capacity, furnace level or troops level
